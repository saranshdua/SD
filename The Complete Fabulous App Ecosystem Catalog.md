# The Complete Fabulous App Ecosystem Catalog

This document provides a comprehensive catalog of the habits, journeys, routines, challenges, and other content available across the entire Fabulous app ecosystem. The information has been compiled from a wide range of sources, including the official Fabulous website and help center, app store listings, blog posts, user forums, and in-depth reviews.

## The Fabulous App Ecosystem

The Fabulous ecosystem consists of a suite of apps designed to improve different aspects of well-being. The main app, **Fabulous**, focuses on habit tracking and routine building, while the other apps target specific areas like nutrition, sleep, and mental health.

| App Name | Focus | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Fabulous** | Habit Tracker & Routine Planner | The core app for building routines, tracking habits, and embarking on guided "Journeys." |
| **Shape** | Healthy Eating & Fitness | Focuses on building healthy eating habits and a positive relationship with exercise. |
| **Lune** | Bedtime Sleep App | Provides tools and content to improve sleep quality and establish a relaxing bedtime routine. |
| **Lumiere** | Anxiety & Stress Aid | Uses evidence-based psychology (ACT, CBT) to help users manage anxiety and stress. |
| **Elixir** | Find Balance & Purpose | Helps users discover their core values and align their actions with a sense of purpose. |
| **Clarify** | ADHD Organizer & Help | Offers tools and coaching specifically designed for users with ADHD to improve focus and organization. |
| **Ambiance** | Ambient Sounds | Provides a library of ambient sounds for focus and relaxation. |
| **Enchant** | Powerful Affirmations | A collection of positive affirmations to support a positive mindset. |

## Fabulous Journeys

Fabulous Journeys are multi-week themed programs that guide users through building a series of related habits. They are structured into three main "Mountains" or phases of development.

### The Three Mountains

1.  **Mountain 1: The Foundation** - Focuses on establishing core, foundational habits for a structured life.
2.  **Mountain 2: The Expansion** - Builds on the foundation with habits related to personal growth, health, and productivity.
3.  **Mountain 3: The Mastery** - Tackles more advanced topics like purpose, relationships, and self-actualization.
